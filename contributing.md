# Contribution Guidelines

Your contributions are welcome. Papers on any use of large language models in finance are in scope, including auditing, accounting, trading, compliance, credit and financial analysis.

## Entry Format

```markdown
- **Paper Title** (Venue, YYYY) [[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/XXXX.XXXXX)] [[Code](https://github.com/owner/repo)]
```

- Use `(arXiv, YYYY.MM)` with the submission month for a paper without a venue.
- Add the `Code` link only for an official repository.
- Put new entries at the top of their section, which keeps each section newest first.

## Corrections

If an entry is wrong or a paper has been accepted somewhere, please open an issue or a pull request.
